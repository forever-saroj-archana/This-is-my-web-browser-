Dono ki mulaqat ek aisi jagah hui jahan dono ne kabhi nahi socha tha ki unhe unka humsafar milega 💫. Pehli mulaqat mein hi Saroj ko Archana ki muskaan 😊 aur bolne ka andaz bohot pasand aaya, aur Archana ko Saroj ki saadgi aur izzat dene wali tabiyat ne impress kiya 🌸.

Dono ki baat-cheet shuru hui 💬. Dheere-dheere chhoti-chhoti baatein lambi conversations mein badal gayi ⏳. Late-night phone calls 🌙📱, ek doosre ke sapno ke baare mein baat karna 🌌, aur har mushkil mein ek doosre ka sath dena 🤝—in sabne unki dosti ko bohot gehra kar diya ❤️. Saroj ko lagne laga ki Archana ke bina uski zindagi adhoori hai 🧩, aur Archana ko bhi Saroj mein ek sachha saathi dikhne laga 💖.
